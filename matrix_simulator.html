LED matrix simulator

Preview how images will look on the 64×32 HUB75 panel before flashing. Drop source images to see them converted the same way as image_matrix_creator.py (approximate resampling), or drop generated include/*_bitmap.h headers to see the exact pixels that will be flashed. Every pixel goes through the firmware's colour path: RGB565 → the library's color565to888 → its CIE 1931 brightness curve.
